## How to read the snapshots

These are manually reviewed public-page captures, not an API feed, checkout quote or promise of present availability. Each published snapshot has the seller URL, exact listing model, timestamp, and known location context. No user ZIP was entered. Some seller pages supplied their own default location, such as Sterling, VA 20166 or New York, NY 10118; these are shown explicitly, not described as national offers. Where page context did not identify a location it is marked not established. Taxes, delivery, installation and location-specific adjustments are not included. No discount arithmetic, conditional rebate subtraction, price-history claim or lowest-price badge was added.

Specifications and product illustrations were not changed. Only price records, explicit listing/location metadata and price-explanation text changed. Existing consent controls, calendar, policies, images, six guides and analytics configuration remain unchanged.

## Evidence

SELLER-PRICE-VERIFICATION.json maps every recorded snapshot to its source URL and saved evidence record. The supporting evidence archive contains public seller page captures. Some public-page fetches returned incomplete content; these were not treated as evidence that a price existed. A bounded browser check of Lowe's confirmed LG WM4000HWA at $899 with seller-default ZIP 20166. It also confirmed that MVW6230HW was out of stock and WT7300CW discontinued; no second active offers were added for them.

No deployment, Vercel token, account change, GitHub push, retailer login, cart or purchase was performed for this review.
